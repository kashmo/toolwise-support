Explore ToolWise

A tool inventory that does more than keep a list.

01 Scan or identify

Add a tool from its box — or from the tool itself.

Still packaged? ToolWise captures the UPC and pre-fills name, brand, category, model, and product image when available. No box and no barcode — most of what is already in your garage? Point the camera and Smart Identify recognizes the tool from a photo, then hands you an editable draft to confirm.

02 A complete record

Keep the details that actually matter.

Store the photo, brand, model, category, location, quantity, condition, battery platform, serial number, and more.

03 Find by location

See every place your tools live.

Browse the garage, basement, workshop, shed, vehicle, storage unit, or locations you create yourself.

04 A smarter collection

Plan purchases and improve your inventory.

Keep a wishlist, understand your collection at a glance, and surface tools missing photos, brands, or locations.

05 Project Advisor

Know whether you can start before you start.

Tell ToolWise what you are working on. Project Advisor lists the tools the whole job usually takes, from start to finish, and marks the ones you own, the tools of yours that may work instead, and the ones you would still need. Pin a project and the count ticks down as you acquire the tools.

Privacy by design

Your tools are your business.

ToolWise does not require a separate ToolWise account. Your inventory and photos are backed up to your personal iCloud account, and your data is not sold.

Free, with Plus when you want it

Your records are always yours.

Adding tools by hand or by voice, searching, barcode checks, and everything you’ve recorded are free. Adding or recognizing tools from a scan comes with a free allowance. ToolWise Plus, an optional subscription billed through Apple at $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year, adds a lending log with return reminders, receipts and manuals kept with each tool, an insurance-ready report, and unlimited scans and project tool lists. With Family Sharing, one subscription covers up to five family members, and each person keeps their own inventory. If Plus ends, nothing you’ve recorded is locked.
